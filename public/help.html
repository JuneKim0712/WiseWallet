Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q.

How do I connect my bank account to the app?

A. Go to Settings > Linked Accounts, select your bank, log in securely, and grant permissions to sync transactions.

Q.

How can I set and track a monthly budget?

A. Go to Budget, tap "Create Budget", set category limits, and the app will track your spending in real time..
