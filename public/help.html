Frequently Asked Questions

How do I edit my transactions?

Click the edit icon next to a transaction, adjust any fields, and press Save.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. We use industry‑standard encryption and conduct regular security audits.

Can I use the app without linking my bank account?

Absolutely. Manual entry lets you track income and expenses with no external connections.

Can I categorize my expenses?

Yes — choose built‑in categories like Food or Rent, or create your own custom tags.

Can I filter reports by category or time period?

Yep — the Reports tab lets you filter by date range, category, or income vs. expense.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings ▸ Export and choose CSV or PDF.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete and follow the prompts.

Is Wise Wallet free to use?

The core app is free. Optional premium tools are clearly marked within the app.
